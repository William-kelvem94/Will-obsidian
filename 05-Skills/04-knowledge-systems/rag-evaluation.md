# RAG Evaluation

## What to check

- retrieval precision;
- retrieval recall;
- chunk quality;
- provenance coverage;
- answer faithfulness;
- latency and cost.

## Common failure modes

- missing the right source;
- over-chunking or under-chunking;
- weak grounding;
- stale knowledge;
- answers that sound right but are not supported.
