# Decision Making

## Useful frame

Good decisions separate:

- signal from noise;
- reversible from irreversible;
- assumptions from evidence;
- short-term pain from long-term gain.

## Decision quality checklist

- problem is clear;
- options are explicit;
- tradeoffs are visible;
- failure modes are named;
- confidence level is stated;
- follow-up is defined.
