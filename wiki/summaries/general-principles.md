# General Principles

## Reusable rules

- keep evidence separate from synthesis;
- optimize for future reuse, not just immediate capture;
- prefer links over duplication;
- maintenance is part of creation;
- measure what changes behavior;
- write notes for a future reader;
- small systems win by consistency;
- big systems win by structure.
