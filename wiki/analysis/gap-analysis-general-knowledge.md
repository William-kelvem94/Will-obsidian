# General Knowledge Gap Analysis

## Biggest gaps

- too many broad directories without enough bridge notes;
- not enough concept pages that can absorb future sources;
- weak linkage between life knowledge and technical knowledge;
- underdeveloped analysis pages in human and practical domains.

## Highest leverage additions

- source summaries by domain;
- concept bridges between domains;
- domain-level hub pages;
- comparison notes;
- question-driven synthesis notes.
