# General Knowledge Growth Model

## Growth phases

- seed: create the first notes for a domain;
- cluster: add adjacent concepts and summaries;
- connect: link to skills, projects, and entities;
- reuse: apply notes in decisions and tasks;
- maintain: refresh stale pages and remove drift.

## Growth metrics

- note count;
- link density;
- ratio of synthesis to raw capture;
- reuse in projects;
- review freshness.
