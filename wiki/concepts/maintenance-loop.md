# Maintenance Loop

Knowledge systems decay unless they are maintained. A maintenance loop makes review part of the design.

## Loop

1. inspect;
2. detect drift;
3. repair links or metadata;
4. refresh stale pages;
5. log the change;
6. remeasure health.
