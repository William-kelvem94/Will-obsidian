# Epistemic Humility

Epistemic humility is the habit of treating knowledge as provisional until it is tested.

## Signals

- explicit confidence levels;
- clear separation between fact and inference;
- willingness to revise beliefs;
- preference for evidence over certainty;
- curiosity without overclaiming.
