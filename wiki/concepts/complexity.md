# Complexity

Complex systems show nonlinearity, emergence, and tradeoffs that do not appear in simple linear models.

## Practical implications

- local optimization can hurt global outcomes;
- small inputs can have large effects;
- delays matter;
- dependencies create hidden coupling;
- good models are often incomplete, not perfect.
