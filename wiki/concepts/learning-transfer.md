# Learning Transfer

Learning transfer is the bridge between memorization and capability. It shows whether a skill can survive a new environment.

## Signals

- faster setup in a new domain;
- fewer mistakes in adjacent tasks;
- better pattern recognition;
- ability to teach the topic back;
- ability to adapt without rigid scripts.
