# Cognitive Biases

Cognitive biases are useful to track because they often explain why smart systems still make poor decisions.

## Common patterns

- confirmation bias;
- availability bias;
- anchoring;
- survivorship bias;
- sunk cost fallacy;
- planning fallacy.

## Mitigations

- write assumptions down;
- compare alternatives;
- use checklists;
- seek disconfirming evidence;
- review decisions after outcomes.
