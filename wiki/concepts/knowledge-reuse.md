# Knowledge Reuse

Reusable knowledge is structured, linkable, and scoped enough to be applied again.

## Reuse signals

- notes answer future questions;
- concepts can be combined;
- examples generalize cleanly;
- maintenance cost stays low;
- provenance remains visible.
