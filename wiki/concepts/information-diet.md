# Information Diet

A qualidade do conhecimento depende da qualidade da entrada. Dieta de informacao e a disciplina de filtrar, limitar e priorizar fontes.

## Principles

- prefer reliable sources;
- reduce repetitive noise;
- separate evidence from opinion;
- capture only what can be reused;
- revisit sources that produce compounding value.

## Risks

- overload;
- shallow collection;
- algorithmic drift;
- false certainty from volume.
