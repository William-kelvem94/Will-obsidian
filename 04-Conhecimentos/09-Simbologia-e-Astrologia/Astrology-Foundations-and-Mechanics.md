# Astrology Foundations and Mechanics

## 1. The Zodiacs
### Tropical Zodiac
- **Reference:** Fixed to the equinoxes.
- **Starting Point:** Vernal Equinox (0° Aries).
- **Focus:** Seasonal cycles and psychological archetypes.

### Sidereal Zodiac
- **Reference:** Fixed to the actual constellations of stars.
- **Ayanamsa:** The difference between Tropical and Sidereal positions (shifts roughly 1° every 72 years due to precession).
- **Focus:** Astronomical precision and karmic structures.

## 2. The Planets (Celestial Bodies)
Each body represents a specific functional drive:
- **Luminaries:** Sun (Core identity, will), Moon (Emotion, subconscious).
- **Personal Planets:** Mercury (Communication, intellect), Venus (Value, attraction), Mars (Action, drive).
- **Social Planets:** Jupiter (Expansion, philosophy), Saturn (Restriction, structure).
- **Outer Planets:** Uranus (Innovation, disruption), Neptune (Dissolution, spirituality), Pluto (Transformation, power).

## 4. Aspects
The geometric relationships between planets, measured by the angle between them.

- **Conjunction (0°):** Fusion of energies, intensity.
- **Sextile (60°):** Harmony, opportunity, ease.
- **Square (90°):** Tension, conflict, growth through friction.
- **Trine (120°):** Natural flow, success, stability.
- **Opposition (180°):** Polarization, projection, balance.
