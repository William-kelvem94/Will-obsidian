# Sacred Geometry and Numerology

## 1. Mathematical Proportions

### Phi ($\phi$) - The Golden Ratio
The Golden Ratio is an irrational number $\phi \approx 1.618\dots$, defined by the ratio:
$$\phi = \frac{1 + \sqrt{5}}{2}$$
It appears in the Fibonacci sequence $F_n = F_{n-1} + F_{n-2}$, where the ratio of consecutive terms converges to $\phi$.

- **Art and Architecture:** Used in the Parthenon and Da Vinci's works to achieve "divine proportion."
- **Nature:** Phyllotaxis (leaf arrangement), nautilus shells, and galactic spirals.

### Pi ($\pi$)
The ratio of a circle's circumference to its diameter $\pi \approx 3.14159\dots$.
In symbolism, $\pi$ represents the infinite, the cyclical nature of time, and the transition from the linear (diameter) to the curved (circumference).

## 2. Geometric Symbolism

### The Vesica Piscis
Formed by the intersection of two circles with the same radius, where the center of each lies on the circumference of the other.
- **Symbolism:** The "womb of the universe," the intersection of spirit and matter.

### The Platonic Solids
Five regular polyhedra where every face is the same regular polygon:
1. **Tetrahedron (Fire):** 4 faces, simplest structure.
2. **Hexahedron/Cube (Earth):** 6 faces, stability.
3. **Octahedron (Air):** 8 faces, balance.
4. **Dodecahedron (Aether/Universe):** 12 faces, cosmic order.
5. **Icosahedron (Water):** 20 faces, flow.

## 3. Numerology as Formal System
Numerology assigns metaphysical meaning to numbers, often treating them as vibrational frequencies.

- **Monad (1):** Unity, the Absolute, the Seed.
- **Dyad (2):** Duality, Polarity, Contrast.
- **Triad (3):** Synthesis, Balance, The Trinity.
- **Tetrad (4):** Manifestation, the Physical World (4 elements, 4 directions).
