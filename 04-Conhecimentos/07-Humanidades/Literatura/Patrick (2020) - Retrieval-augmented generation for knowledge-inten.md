## 📝 Resumo do Artigo (Abstract)
> Large pre-trained language models have been shown to store impressive amounts of semantic knowledge. However, their ability to access and precisely manipulate knowledge is still limited. We introduce Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) to bridge this gap.
